A state-of-the-art facility, unique in its kind.

Promoted by two highly qualified technocrats with over 25 years of entrepreneurial experience, Biomed International is equipped with a comprehensive laboratory featuring Shimadzu HPLC, UV spectrophotometer, polarimeter, stability chambers, and an exclusive microbiology lab.

We are certified by FSSAI, HALAL and ISO, and are currently working toward WHO-GMP certification. Our products are supplied to major domestic nutraceutical, food and pharmaceutical companies, and exported to various countries.

Infrastructure

Two units in Tirupati District.

150,000 sq. ft. across four acres, surrounded by green.

Unit-I houses an administration block, two production blocks, a warehouse, R&D and quality control laboratories, and utility blocks. A dedicated cleanroom environment supports pharmaceutical, nutraceutical and food supplement manufacturing.

The plant is located 10 km from National Highway 45 near Guduru Junction, and 150 km from Chennai International Airport and Seaport.
