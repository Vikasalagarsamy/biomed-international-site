Spray drying technology

Optimising product quality with efficient spray drying.

Spray drying enables drying in a single operation with minimal human contact, ensuring safe and efficient handling. It allows precise control over particle size and shape, producing uniform, highly concentrated powders with excellent solubility.

Designed for sterile environments, spray dryers are ideal for heat-sensitive or oxidisable substances — the resulting powders can also be easily compressed into tablet forms.

The technique atomises solutions, slurries, or emulsions into fine droplets, which are then dried by controlled hot air to form solid powders — simplifying both handling and packaging. Due to its efficiency and reliability, spray drying is widely used across food and pharmaceutical industries.
