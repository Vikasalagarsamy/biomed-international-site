Natural raw materials (bovine cartilage, hide, fish skin, chitin and bile) are extracted, purified, crystallised and dried in our own two units in Andhra Pradesh, so every batch is consistent and traceable to its source.

Capabilities

From basic raw material to finished ingredient

Every stage runs in-house, from the raw material that arrives at our gate to the sealed drum that leaves it.

01

Extraction & hydrolysis

Enzymatic and acid hydrolysis of cartilage, hide, fish skin, chitin and bile.

02

Purification & crystallisation

Filtration, precipitation, decolourisation and repeated crystallisation.

03

Drying

Spray dryers for peptides and chondroitin; an agitated Nutsche filter dryer for crystalline products.

04

Quality control

HPLC, GC, UV-Vis spectrophotometry and a 21 CFR Part 11-compliant digital polarimeter.

05

Stability & R&D

Stability chambers at several storage conditions and an in-house R&D lab.

06

Custom specifications

Molecular weight, particle size and assay matched to your formulation.
