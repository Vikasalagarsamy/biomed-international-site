Capabilities

We start where the others don't.

Chondroitin Sulphate reaches the Indian market in one of two ways. Almost every supplier imports it, or picks the process up somewhere in the middle. We are the only company in India, as of now, making it from the base raw material stage through to the finished product.

Why it matters

When you start at the beginning, the specification is yours to control rather than inherited from someone else's process — and every batch is traceable end to end, back to the raw material it came from.
