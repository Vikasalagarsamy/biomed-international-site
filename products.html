Pharmaceutical · API

Chondroitin Sulphate Sodium

CAS9082-07-9
GradesUSP · BP
India’s only drug-licensed manufacturer
Made in-house fromBovine cartilage → finished USP/BP powder

Chondroitin sulphate is a glycosaminoglycan: a long chain of alternating sugar units that holds water inside cartilage and helps it absorb load. We isolate it from bovine cartilage, then purify it through enzymatic hydrolysis, filtration and precipitation. The result is a pharmaceutical-grade API: a free-flowing, off-white powder that dissolves readily and presses well into tablets and capsules.

Food ingredient

Collagen Peptides

CAS9064-67-9
SourcesBovine · Fish
Made in-house fromBovine hide and fish skin → food-grade, spray-dried peptide powder

Collagen is the main structural protein in skin, bone and connective tissue. We break it down with controlled enzymatic hydrolysis into short peptides of low molecular weight, then spray-dry them. The powder is neutral in taste and odour, dissolves clear in cold water and stays stable in drinks, bars and capsules. Collagen peptides are a food ingredient, so they fit food, beverage and supplement formulations alike.

Nutraceutical

Glucosamine Hydrochloride

CAS66-84-2
GradeUSP
Made in-house fromChitin → glucosamine HCl → sulphate 2KCl and 2NaCl salts

Glucosamine is an amino sugar that the body uses to build the glycosaminoglycans in cartilage. We hydrolyse chitin with acid, and decolourise and crystallise the product into glucosamine hydrochloride, a high-purity white powder. From our own glucosamine HCl we also make the two sulphate salts, so formulators can choose the one that fits their label and market.

Key starting material for UDCA

Cholic Acid

CAS81-25-4
SourceBovine bile
Made in-house fromBovine bile → purified crystalline acid, a key starting material for UDCA

Cholic acid is a primary bile acid. The liver produces it from cholesterol, and it helps the body digest and absorb fats. We recover it from bovine bile by saponification, then purify it by repeated crystallisation. Its main use is as the key starting material (KSM) for ursodeoxycholic acid (UDCA), a widely used liver and gallbladder API.
