About Biomed International

Built by technocrats. Driven by chemistry. Focused on biomolecules.

Biomed International is a specialised manufacturer of high-quality biomolecules for the pharmaceutical, nutraceutical and food industries.

Our story

Started with one molecule. Grown into four.

Biomed began with Chondroitin Sulphate Sodium and is India’s only drug-licensed manufacturer of it. The portfolio has since grown to include Collagen Peptides, Glucosamine Hydrochloride and Cholic Acid.

  1. 2014

    Company established

    Biomed International is incorporated.

  2. 2016

    Operations begin at Unit I

    Production starts at Mekanuru, Guduru.

  3. Expansion

    Unit II and a wider portfolio

    Unit II is commissioned at Attivaram as the portfolio grows.

Built on experience

Led by its founders

Each of Biomed’s two founders brings more than 25 years of entrepreneurial experience in manufacturing and the life-science sector.

That experience, together with deep technical knowledge of biomolecule manufacturing, shapes how Biomed approaches quality, process excellence and reliable supply.

Looking ahead

Ready for the biomolecules of tomorrow

From a single product to a growing portfolio, Biomed has built its capabilities through technical expertise, integrated manufacturing and continued investment in infrastructure.

Two manufacturing units, experienced leadership, in-house R&D and quality capabilities, and room to expand give Biomed a strong base to serve its current customers and to develop the next generation of biomolecules.
