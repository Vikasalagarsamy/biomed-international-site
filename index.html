Pioneers in green biotechnology

Our state-of-the-art facility is unique in its kind, promoted by two highly qualified technocrats with over 25 years of entrepreneurial experience.

Biomed International is equipped with a comprehensive laboratory featuring Shimadzu HPLC, UV spectrophotometer, polarimeter, stability chambers, and an exclusive microbiology lab. We are the only company in India to have obtained a drug license for Chondroitin Sulfate Sodium.

We are certified by FSSAI, HALAL, and ISO, and are currently working toward WHO-GMP certification. Our products are supplied to major domestic nutraceutical, food, and pharmaceutical companies, and exported to various countries.
